Privacy

Overview. Homemade Breathing is a guided breathing app for families. It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Data storage

Your settings, best times and breathing history stay on your device only, in the app's own storage. Nothing is sent to external servers or third parties.

Permissions

The app works entirely offline. It asks for permission to send notifications only if you switch on daily reminders, and those reminders are scheduled on the device itself. It does not access your contacts, location, photos, microphone or health data.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for the whole family. It collects no personal information from anyone, and contains no advertising, analytics, in-app purchases or tracking of any kind.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at contact@homemade-stories.com.
