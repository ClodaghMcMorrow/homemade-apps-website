Homemade Drawings

Small hands, big ideas.

Open it and there is a shelf of pictures and a big button for a new page. Tap it and the whole screen becomes paper. No menus to learn, no account to make, nothing to unlock — just draw.

Launching soon on the App Store.

What's inside

1

Draw

Pencil, marker, paint, glow and spray, each with its own styles — hold a brush to see how else it can draw. A soft eraser paints the paper back on.

2

Decorate

A sheet of stickers — faces, animals, weather, food and more — that land with their own little tilt, and shapes that come out of a drag, outlined or filled in.

3

Multiply

Mirror, four-ways and mandala symmetry turn one stroke into a pattern. Coloured paper from cream to midnight blue, and glow that really glows on the dark ones.

4

Keep

Pictures are saved as they are drawn and kept on the shelf, ready to be finished another day, renamed or shared. Undo takes back one thing at a time — even clearing the page.

Need help?

Settings, saving and sharing, and answers to the questions parents ask most.
