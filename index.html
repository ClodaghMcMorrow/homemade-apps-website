My promise to you

Every app I make follows the same principles. No exceptions, no "freemium" editions, no dark patterns. Just software that respects you and your family.

  1. No ads. Ever.

    I will never show advertisements in any of my apps — not now, not in a future update.

  2. Pay once, own it.

    A single purchase gets you the full app, all future updates, and no renewal reminders.

  3. No account.

    Download, open, use. No sign-up form, no email required, no password to forget.

  4. Your data stays yours.

    Nothing leaves your device. I don't collect, store, or sell any personal data.

  5. Kid-safe by design.

    No addictive loops, no notifications clamouring for attention, no in-app purchases hidden behind a cheerful button.

  6. Made for families.

    Every decision starts with the same question: is this good for the families using it?

  7. No internet needed.

    Every app runs entirely on your device. Play in the car, on a plane, or anywhere with no signal — nothing ever needs a connection.
